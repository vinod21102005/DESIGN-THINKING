Scientific Perspective: Are Classic Somatotypes Outdated?

The three textbook types we started from.

Ectomorph

Small bone structure, naturally lean, fast metabolism. Struggles to gain weight without a high calorie surplus.

Mesomorph

Naturally athletic, high muscle mass density, efficient metabolic recovery. Gains lean mass easily.

Endomorph

Larger bone frame, higher body fat density, slower carbohydrate clearance. Prone to easy fat storage.

Design Gap Identified: 83% of users fall into hybrid classifications. Treating somatotypes as static rigid buckets leads to plateau and injury.

Advanced Profiling: Mapping Hybrid Body Architectures

Three hybrids the platform recognizes and plans for.

Ecto-Mesomorph

Lean framework with high upper-body muscle response.

Strategy: High-intensity strength training + moderate carb cycling.

Meso-Endomorph

Muscular foundation with rapid subcutaneous fat accumulation.

Strategy: Macro timing, metabolic conditioning, and low-glycemic fuel.

Ecto-Endomorph (Skinny Fat)

Slim limbs with central/visceral fat storage.

Strategy: Metabolic rehab, progressive resistance, and insulin sensitivity stabilization.
